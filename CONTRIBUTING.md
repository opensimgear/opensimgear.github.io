# External contributions paused

OpenSimGear is temporarily not accepting external creative contributions while
its licensing and contribution policies are reviewed. Development continues
under the maintainer's control.

Please do not submit pull requests, patches, code, firmware, hardware designs,
schematics, CAD files, documentation changes, translations, artwork or other
creative contributions. Unsolicited submissions will not be accepted for inclusion
during this pause. No contributor agreement or transfer of rights is implied by
sending material.

## Bug reports and questions

Factual bug reports and questions remain welcome through the repository's issue
tracker where enabled, or info@opensimgear.org. Describe the symptoms, environment,
expected behavior and actual behavior. Do not include patches, replacement code,
design files or other proposed creative contributions.

For security reports, follow any SECURITY.md instructions in this repository.
Do not post credentials or other private information.

## Current licensing

Original OpenSimGear material is all rights reserved while licensing is under
review. Public access does not grant a personal, noncommercial or commercial
reuse license. See [LICENSE](LICENSE) and
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Contact legal@opensimgear.org for permission requests. This pause will remain in
place until the maintainer explicitly announces a new contribution policy.
